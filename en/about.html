The story

I come from graphic design: I graduated in Graphic Design and Communication at the I.T.T. Nervi Galilei in Altamura, with 92/100. There I learned how to compose an image and what makes the eye stop; since 2021 I've been doing it with a stills camera and a video camera.

The name changed as I did. In 2021 gh0st.ph was born, in early 2025 it became produkts.work and since July 2025 it has been produkts.it: the name I sign my work with, from Altamura.

In October 2025 I joined AMARCORD, the Mediterranean Film Academy in Bari, where I became a certified director of photography. There I worked with film professionals such as Raffaele Mertes, Massimo Giuliani, Tiziana Lattuca and Giuseppe Maldera, and met the people I now make short films, feature films and more with.

I look for stories that aren't explained: they're watched. And for people who believe in them enough to turn a wardrobe into a control room.
